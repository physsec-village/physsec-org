Bypass
Simulator Games

Learning about physical security bypass is a hands-on experience. These interactive games and demos let you explore and test various physical security exploits right in your browser. A keyboard and mouse are required.

These games were originally created for online conferences during the COVID-19 pandemic, when hands-on physical security education had to move online. They remain freely available as educational tools for the community.
