FAQ

The Physical Security Village (or PSV) is a not-for-profit dedicated to physical security education! We most commonly appear as a “village” at security conferences, where you can come by our room and try our hands-on exhibits covering topics like bypass techniques, access control, RFID, lockpicking, and remediations!

We have many educational hands-on exhibits, where you can learn about various physical security topics. These include motion sensor demonstrations and vehicular security setups, but our mainstay exhibits are our mini-doors with all sorts of commonly seen door hardware – you’ll learn common vulnerabilities such as improperly protected latches, door handles and knobs that can be used from the other side of the door, and how alarm systems can be defeated and surveillance systems avoided.
